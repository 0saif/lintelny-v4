Local Expertise

Pre-War Housing Stock

Much of the Bronx housing stock dates from the 1920s–1950s. We regularly encounter galvanized plumbing, knob-and-tube wiring, and original cast-iron radiator systems. Our pre-construction assessments catch these conditions before they affect your budget.

NYC DOB Bronx Office

Permits for Bronx projects are filed at the NYC DOB Bronx Borough Office at 1932 Arthur Avenue. We know permit timelines and inspectors for this borough and manage all filings from ALT2 through sign-off.

Mixed Residential Building Types

The Bronx has one of the more varied housing mixes in NYC — from co-op towers in Co-op City to attached rowhouses in Fordham to detached homes in Riverdale. Our scopes are written to the actual conditions of your unit, not a template for a generic building type.
