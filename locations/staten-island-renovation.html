Local Expertise

Detached Homes & Larger Footprints

Staten Island has a higher proportion of detached single-family homes than the other four boroughs. Larger kitchens, finished basements, and attached garages are common scope items here that rarely come up in Manhattan or Brooklyn apartments.

NYC DOB Staten Island Office

Permits for Staten Island projects are filed at the NYC DOB Staten Island Borough Office at 10 Richmond Terrace. We manage all DOB filings, inspections, and sign-offs as standard scope — not an add-on.

Roofing & Exterior Work

Staten Island's larger home footprints and pitched roof prevalence make it one of our stronger roofing markets. We handle architectural shingle installations, gutters, and full roofing replacements under the same written contract standards as our interior work.
