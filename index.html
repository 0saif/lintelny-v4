03 — Process

How we work.

01
Consultation
Free on-site assessment. We measure, photograph, and review your building's specific rules. No sales pressure — just information gathering for an honest scope and price.
02
Scope & Estimate
Written scope of work with line-item pricing. No vague allowances. You see exactly what you are paying for, broken down by trade and material, before you commit to anything.
03
Contract
NYC HIC-compliant contract with full scope, payment schedule, timeline, and warranty terms. You read it. We review it together. Then we sign.
04
Execution
Daily progress updates. Named project lead. Scheduled inspections. We manage the subcontractors, the permits, and the timeline so you do not have to.
05
Closeout
Formal punch list walkthrough. Every item addressed before final payment. Signed closeout documentation. 2-year labor warranty on all work.
