Co-op & Condo Renovation

Co-op and condo renovation is defined as much by the building as by the finishes. Management rules, approvals, insurance requirements, and delivery logistics shape the entire job. We manage the paperwork so you focus on design decisions.

Project Phases

How it happens.

01
Board Package
Complete alteration agreement package including scope, plans, insurance certificates, contractor credentials.
02
Board Review
Typical approval 4-8 weeks. We respond to all board questions within 24 hours.
03
Pre-Construction
Building walk-through with super. Elevator schedule reserved. Protection installed.
04
Construction
Work executed within building hours. Daily communication with super. Subcontractors briefed on house rules.
05
Close-Out
Building final walk-through. DOB inspections. Security deposit release documentation.
