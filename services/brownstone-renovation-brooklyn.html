Project Phases

How it happens.

01
Assessment
Structural engineer inspection. Environmental testing. Full condition report. This phase determines your real budget.
02
Remediation
Lead and asbestos removed by certified crews. Clearance testing before renovation begins.
03
Structural & Systems
Any structural repairs first. Then plumbing risers, electrical service upgrade, HVAC.
04
Renovation
Kitchen, bathrooms, flooring, drywall, paint. Finish work starts once systems are inspected.
05
Facade & Close-Out
Exterior work if in scope. DOB final inspections. LPC sign-off if applicable.
