Project Phases

How it happens.

01
Demo & Rough-In
Existing kitchen removed. Plumbing and electrical rough-in per new layout.
02
Cabinet Install
Cabinets delivered, installed, leveled. Hardware installed.
03
Countertop Template
Fabricator templates on-site. 10-14 day fabrication follows.
04
Finishing
Backsplash, flooring, paint, trim completed before countertops arrive.
05
Countertop & Appliances
Countertops installed and sealed. Sink plumbed. All appliances connected and tested.
06
Sign-Off
DOB final inspection. Punch list walkthrough. Warranty documentation.
