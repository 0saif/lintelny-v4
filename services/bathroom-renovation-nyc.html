Project Phases

How it happens.

01
Demo & Rough-In
Existing fixtures removed. Plumbing and electrical rough-in completed. DOB inspection scheduled.
02
Waterproofing
Full membrane system installed and flood-tested for 24 hours before any tile goes down. Non-negotiable.
03
Tile Installation
Floor tile first, then wall tile. All cuts, niches, and trim pieces. Grout and seal.
04
Fixtures & Vanity
Vanity plumbed. Toilet installed. Shower trim and glass. All connections tested.
05
Final Sign-Off
DOB final inspection. Full punch list walkthrough. Warranty documentation delivered.
