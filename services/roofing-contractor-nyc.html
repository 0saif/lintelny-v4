FAQ

Common questions.

If your roof is flat or low-slope (less than 2:12 pitch), you need a membrane system. Standard pitch gets architectural shingles. We inspect and recommend.
In NYC, like-for-like replacement on 1-2 family homes typically does not require a permit. Structural changes may require one. We confirm at estimate.
Flat roof: 10-year labor warranty. Pitched shingles: 5-year labor warranty. Manufacturer material warranties are separate (25-50 years).
